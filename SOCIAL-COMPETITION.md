# Social competition

There are two separate $500 social prizes: one for LinkedIn and one for X.

You can enter either prize or both. Entries close at 17:00.

Social entries are individual, even if you are building the hackathon agent as part of a team. Each submitted post belongs to one named person.

To qualify for either prize, you must share both official OpenStocks launch posts:

1. Retweet or repost the OpenStocks launch post on X.
2. Repost the OpenStocks launch post on LinkedIn.

You can add your competition entry to those shares or publish a separate, more detailed post on the platform you want to enter.

The post you enter must include every required tag listed below. If any required tag is missing, that post will not qualify for the relevant platform prize.

## X prize

1. Retweet or quote-post the official launch post from [@_openstocks](https://x.com/_openstocks).
2. Either add your competition entry to the quote post or publish it separately.
3. In the post being judged, tag all four accounts:
   - [@_openstocks](https://x.com/_openstocks)
   - [@primerapp_](https://x.com/primerapp_)
   - [@AITinkerers](https://x.com/AITinkerers)
   - [@openai](https://x.com/openai)
4. Submit the URL of the post you want judged before 17:00.

## LinkedIn prize

1. Repost the official OpenStocks launch post on LinkedIn.
2. Either add your competition entry to the repost or publish it separately.
3. In the post being judged, tag:
   - [Primer](https://www.linkedin.com/company/104235791)
   - [OpenAI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/openai/)
   - [AI Tinkerers](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ai-tinkerers/)
4. Submit the URL of the post you want judged before 17:00.

You do not need to tag OpenStocks separately on LinkedIn; sharing the official OpenStocks post is the requirement.

## What makes a good entry

A simple promotional repost is not enough on its own. The post being judged should add a thoughtful contribution—for example, how you approached building the agent, something you learned, or your view on how agents may change the industry.

The judges look for:

1. **Original insight and thoughtfulness.** Teach people something useful about how you built the agent, what you learned or how agents may change the industry.
2. **Specificity and authenticity.** Refer to your real experience and choices rather than posting generic event promotion.
3. **Clarity and relevance.** Make the idea easy to understand and relevant to the event or the wider industry.

Follower count and raw engagement do not decide the winner. Engagement may be used only as a tie-break between posts the judges consider equally strong.

The direct launch-post links and entry form appear on [openstocks.com/hackathon](https://openstocks.com/hackathon) on Sunday. Sign in with your own Google email before sending the post links. A corrected form from the same account replaces the earlier one before 17:00.

By submitting the social entry form, you accept these social competition and prize rules.
