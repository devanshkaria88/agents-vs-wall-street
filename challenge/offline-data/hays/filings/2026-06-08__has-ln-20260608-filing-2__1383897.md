# Transaction in Own Shares
RNS Number : 4143H

Hays PLC

08 June 2026

Transaction in own shares

Hays plc (the "Company") announces that during the period between 01 June 2026 and 05 June 2026 it has purchased the following number of its ordinary shares of 1p each on the London Stock Exchange through BNP Paribas SA as part of its treasury share purchase programme announced on 1 June 2026.

| Date of purchase | Number of ordinary shares purchased | Lowest price paid per share | Highest price paid per share | Volume weighted average price paid per share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 June 2026 | 337,336 | 33.0200 | 33.9000 | 33.7240 |
| 02 June 2026 | 867,000 | 33.8400 | 34.5000 | 34.1504 |
| 03 June 2026 | 1,597,137 | 33.1000 | 34.0800 | 33.6034 |
| 04 June 2026 | 1,700,000 | 33.7600 | 34.9200 | 34.4423 |
| 05 June 2026 | 1,210,863 | 34.6200 | 35.3000 | 35.1178 |

The Company intends to hold the purchased shares in treasury. Following the purchase and settlement of these shares, the Company will hold 6,860,797 of its ordinary shares in treasury and will have 1,593,572,295 ordinary shares in issue (excluding treasury shares).

In accordance with Article 5(1)(b) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 as it applies in the UK (the Market Abuse Regulation), a full breakdown of the individual purchases is attached to this announcement.
