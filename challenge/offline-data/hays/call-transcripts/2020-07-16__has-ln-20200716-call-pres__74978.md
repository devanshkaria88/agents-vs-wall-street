Unknown speaker: Net fees in the quarter declined by 34%, with all of our markets greatly impacted by the pandemic. Temp net fees were down 26% and perm down 44%. One, conditions in each region were extremely tough. Unknown speaker: At the specialism level, IT, which represented 29% of group net fees in the quarter, was relatively resilient, with fees only down 17%. Also by group headcount down 9% in the quarter. Additionally, at the 30th of June, 18% of our group employees were either in job support schemes, short-time working arrangements, or had voluntarily reduced their pay, including senior management. Unknown speaker: Three, while we will continue to tightly manage our costs, we expect our periodic cost base to increase as we enter FY 2021.
