## Transaction Overview

- -Deere to acquire Deere-Hitachi joint venture businesses
- Deere will acquire three joint venture factories and workforce
- Deere will acquire intellectual property license in perpetuity for continued manufacturing of current Deere-branded models
- -Deere will enter long-term supply agreement with Hitachi to source and manufacture the current products at existing locations
- -Hitachi Construction Machinery will assume distribution of Hitachi-branded excavators and mining in the Americas
- -$275M transaction consideration for acquisition of factories and intellectual property
- -Transaction consideration at closing will be net of cash and working capital
- -Margin &amp; EPS accretive in year one
- -Expected close in FY22
