### # **Deere Use-of-Cash Priorities **

*Image: The image is a flowchart illustrating a company's capital allocation strategy, starting with "Cash from Operations" at the top. This cash is then used to maintain a "Committed to 'A' Rating," which involves managing the balance sheet and liquidity to ensure access to low-cost funding. Next, the cash is directed towards "Fund Operating and Growth Needs," specifically for value-creating investments in the company's businesses.
